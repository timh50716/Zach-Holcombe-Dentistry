Welcome to Our Practice

We believe great dental care starts with getting to know our patients. Our practice is focused on providing personalized, comfortable care for the whole family in a welcoming environment. We take the time to listen, answer questions and build lasting relationships with our patients while helping them maintain healthy, confident smiles for years to come.

Dr. Holcombe took ownership of the practice in 2018, continuing the legacy of care built by Dr. Glenn Miller. As a small, family-owned practice, you’ll see the same dentist at every visit.
