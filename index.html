Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about new patient visits, insurance, emergencies and more.

Is Dr. Holcombe accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients of all ages are always welcome. Request an appointment online or call 828-684-3839.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, X-rays and a cleaning, so Dr. Holcombe can get a complete picture of your dental health. For new adult patients, the comprehensive exam is $225. See our new patient information.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We file all dental insurance claims for you. We are an out-of-network provider for every insurance plan, and many patients with dental benefits still choose to see us. Call our office with your plan information and we’ll help you understand your coverage. Learn more about insurance.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

Yes. Flexible financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry, so you can get the care you need now and pay over time.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have a toothache, a broken tooth or a lost crown, call 828-684-3839 and we’ll do our best to see you as quickly as possible. New patients with urgent needs are welcome. Learn more about emergency dentistry.

Do you see children?

Yes. We’re a family practice and care for patients of all ages, from young children to senior adults. Learn more about children’s dentistry.
