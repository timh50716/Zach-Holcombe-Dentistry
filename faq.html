Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about new patient visits, insurance, financing, emergencies and our services.

New Patients & Scheduling

Is Dr. Holcombe accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients of all ages are always welcome. Request an appointment online or call 828-684-3839.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, X-rays and a cleaning, so Dr. Holcombe can get a complete picture of your dental health. For new adult patients, the comprehensive exam is $225. See our new patient information.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call our office at 828-684-3839 or send an appointment request online. Online requests aren’t confirmed until our office calls you to set a time.

What are your office hours?

We’re open Monday through Thursday, 8:00am to 5:00pm. The office is closed Friday through Sunday.

Where is your office?

We’re at 1944 Hendersonville Rd., Suite B-2, Asheville, NC 28803, in South Asheville. Get directions and see the map.

Insurance & Financing

Do you accept dental insurance?

We file all dental insurance claims for you. We are an out-of-network provider for every insurance plan, and many patients with dental benefits still choose to see us. Call our office with your plan information and we’ll help you understand your coverage. Learn more about insurance.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

Yes. Flexible financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry, so you can get the care you need now and pay over time.

Our Care & Services

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have a toothache, a broken tooth or a lost crown, call 828-684-3839 and we’ll do our best to see you as quickly as possible. New patients with urgent needs are welcome. Learn more about emergency dentistry.

Do you see children?

Yes. We’re a family practice and care for patients of all ages, from young children to senior adults. Learn more about children’s dentistry.

Will I see the same dentist at every visit?

Yes. We’re a small, family-owned practice, so you’ll see Dr. Holcombe at every visit and he’ll get to know you and your smile over time.

Does Dr. Holcombe place dental implants?

Dr. Holcombe provides implant restorations — the crowns, bridges and dentures that attach to implants. The implant post is placed by a surgical specialist, and our office helps coordinate your care.

What dental services do you offer?

We offer cleanings and checkups, children’s dentistry, crowns, bridges and fillings, cosmetic dentistry, emergency care, implant restorations, dentures, root canals, tooth extractions, gum disease treatment and TMJ treatment and night guards.

Have a question about a specific treatment? Each of our service pages has its own FAQ section.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is happy to help. Request an appointment or give us a call.
