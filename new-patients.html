New Patients

New smiles are always welcome! Here’s how to get ready for your first visit.

Your First Visit

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, X-rays and a cleaning. We’ll get to know you, review your health history and answer your questions. Explore all of our dental services.

New Patient Special

$225

Adult comprehensive exam for new patients. Call or request an appointment to schedule.

  1. Request an Appointment

    Request an appointment online or call 828-684-3839, Monday–Thursday, 8:00am–5:00pm.

  2. Complete Your Forms

    Download the patient information form below and fill it out at home to save time when you arrive.

  3. Visit Our Office

    Find us at 1944 Hendersonville Rd., Suite B-2, Asheville, NC 28803. We look forward to getting to know you!

Insurance & Financing

We want cost to be one less thing to worry about.

Dental Insurance

We file all dental insurance claims for you. We’re an out-of-network provider for every insurance plan, and many patients with dental benefits still choose to see us. Call our office with your plan information and we’ll be glad to help you understand your coverage.

Financing

Flexible payment options are available through CareCredit and Cherry, so you can get the care you need now and pay over time.
