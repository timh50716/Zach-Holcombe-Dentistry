Cosmetic Dentistry in Asheville, NC

Teeth whitening, tooth-colored fillings, veneers, crowns and bridges to help you smile with confidence.

We understand the importance of being able to smile with confidence. A great smile is one of the best ways to make a positive impression, which is why we offer a wide variety of treatments to help give you the smile you want.

Every cosmetic plan starts with a conversation about your goals and an exam to make sure your teeth and gums are healthy enough to support the result you want.

Cosmetic Treatments We Offer

Teeth Whitening

Whitening helps you have a brighter, whiter smile. Teeth naturally change color over time and also become discolored from coffee, tea, red wine and smoking. Whitening is not for every situation, but we would love to discuss whether it could benefit you.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

We offer resin composite fillings that blend in with your natural tooth, so repairs from cavities or small chips are hard to notice.

Veneers

Veneers cover the front surface of a tooth to change its shape or color. They can improve teeth that are stained, chipped, worn or slightly uneven.

Crowns

A crown covers and protects a cracked, damaged or heavily filled tooth, restoring its strength and appearance. Crowns are also commonly recommended after a root canal on a back tooth.

Bridges

A bridge spans the gap where a tooth is missing, anchored to the teeth on each side. It replaces the missing tooth and makes chewing easier. A bridge can also be supported by dental implants instead of natural teeth.

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Right for You?

You may benefit from a cosmetic consultation if you have:

  • Stained or yellowed teeth
  • Chipped, cracked or worn teeth
  • Teeth that are uneven or oddly shaped
  • A gap from a missing tooth
  • Older fillings that are dark or easy to see

If we find decay or gum disease during your exam, we’ll treat that first through preventive care or gum treatment so your cosmetic work lasts.

Caring for Your Results

  • Brush twice daily and clean between your teeth every day.
  • After whitening, limit coffee, tea, red wine and tobacco to keep your shade longer.
  • Avoid chewing ice, pens or other hard objects that can chip teeth and restorations.
  • Keep up with regular checkups so we can monitor crowns, bridges, veneers and fillings.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Frequently Asked Questions

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

Whitening under a dentist’s guidance is a common and generally safe way to brighten natural teeth. Some people have temporary sensitivity. We’ll check that whitening is appropriate for your teeth before you start.

Will whitening change the color of my crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening works on natural tooth enamel and does not change the color of crowns, veneers or fillings. If you have visible restorations, we’ll talk through how to keep everything matching.

What’s the difference between a veneer and a crown?

A veneer covers only the front surface of a tooth and is mainly used to improve appearance. A crown covers the entire tooth and is used when a tooth needs extra strength and protection as well.

Can you replace my old silver fillings with tooth-colored ones?

Often, yes. Dr. Holcombe will check your existing fillings and let you know whether replacing them with tooth-colored composite makes sense.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

With good home care and regular checkups, crowns and bridges can last many years. How long depends on your bite, habits and oral hygiene.
